# VocabApp: Master System & Identity Roadmap

## 1. Core Identity (Ferhat Hoca & Flamingo)
- **Voice**: Wise, sincere, and encouraging English teacher assistant. [cite: 2026-02-04]
- **Philosophy**: "Less is More". Avoid fluff; use strong verbs and direct sentences. [cite: 2025-12-07]
- **Aesthetic**: Mascot (Flamingo) guides the user through "Mind Bonds".

## 2. Global Discovery Limit (The 12 Rule)
- **Quota**: Daily limit of 12 global "Stranger" (New) cards. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Application**: The limit applies across all modes (Vocab + PV). [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Review Policy**: Cards in "Acquaintance" or higher status (Reviews) are UNLIMITED. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Indicator**: A "Daily Discovery Bar" showing progress (e.g., 5/12) using Flamingo feather icons.

## 3. The Mind Bonds System (XP-Driven)
Statüler sadece zamana değil, "Bağ Puanı" (Bond XP) ve SM-2 verilerine dayanır. [cite: 2026-03-01]

### Scoring Matrix (Implicit)
- **5 (Mastered)**: Swipe Right without opening details. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **4 (Got it)**: Swipe Right after viewing details/cases. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **2 (Remind Me)**: Swipe Left after viewing details/cases (Amber glow). [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **0 (New to Me)**: Swipe Left without viewing details (Indigo/Grey glow). [cite: 2026-03-01]

### Status Progression
- **Stranger (Yabancı)**: $XP: 0$. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Acquaintance (Tanış)**: $0 < XP < 100$. (Sub-levels 1, 2, 3 based on XP). [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Confidant (Sırdaş)**: $XP \ge 100$ AND $Interval \ge 7$ days. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Companion (Yoldaş)**: $XP \ge 250$ AND $Interval \ge 21$ days OR 5 perfect quiz streaks. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Stubborn (Dirençli)**: Special status for cards with score 2. [cite: 2026-03-01]

## 4. Mode Architectures
### Vocab & PV Modes (Active Learning)
- Uses SM-2 algorithm. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- $I(n) = I(n-1) \times EF$. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- Integrated "Case Stories" and "Sentence Builder" quizzes.

### Chill Mode (The Library)
- **Purpose**: Passive reading and archiving. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Content**: FULL copy of Phrasal Verbs and Vocab data (Definitions, Stories, Examples). [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Rules**: NO Daily Limit. NO SM-2 impact. No quizzes. [cite: 2026-03-01]
- **Sync**: Cards swiped here are marked as "Seen" but do not trigger learning cycles unless added to a Deck. [cite: 2026-03-01]

## 5. UI/UX Rules
- **Visual Rewards**: Every XP gain triggers a slight "glow" on the Flamingo's feather.
- **Fuzzy Validation**: Sentence Builder quizzes must allow adverb/conjunction flexibility.
